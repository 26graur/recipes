Bacon Wrapped Brussel Sprouts

    Ingredients

  • Bacon
  • Brussel sprouts
  • Tooth picks

    Steps

  1. Clean the brussel sprouts
  2. Wrap the bacon around the brussel sprout and use a tooth pick to secure it
  3. Place bacon wrapped brussel sprout s on a tray and place in the oven for ten minutes to gain your desired crispyness
