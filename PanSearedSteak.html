Pan Seared Steak

    Ingredients

  1. Steak
    • New York Steak
    • Filet Mignon
    • Sirlion
    • T-bone
    • Ribeye
  2. Salt
  3. Pepper
  4. Your choice of seasoning

    Steps

  1. Pat dry
  2. Season with salt and pepper
  3. Preheat pan and use 1/2 tbs oil to make sure the steak won't stick
  4. Sear steak on both sides for 3-4 minutes each
  5. Use tongs to sear the steak on each side for 1 minute
  6. Add any other desired seasonings
  7. Remove from the pan and let sit for aproximatly 10 minutes before cutting
