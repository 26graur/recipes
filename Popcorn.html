Popcorn

    Ingedients

  1. Popcorn kennals
  2. Oil
  3. Salt
  4. Butter

    Steps

  1. Place a small amount of oil in a pot or pan
  2. Place the kennals in the pot or pan
  3. Cover the pot or pan and turn oven top to high
  4. Shift the pot or pan around
  5. Wait for the popping noise from the pot or pan to stop then transfer the popcorn to bowl
  6. Add your desired amount of salt and butter
