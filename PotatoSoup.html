Potato Soup

    Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Bacon
  • Your choice of Vegetables
  • Your choice of Cheese
    • Cheddar is recommended
  • Chicken broth
  • Flour
  • Milk

    Steps

  1. Clean the potatoes
  2. Cut the potatoes into chunks
  3. Cut the bacon into your desired size pieces
  4. Mix a cup of milk and a half cup of flour into a pot
  5. Place the potato chunks, bacon pieces and your choice of vegitables into the pot
  6. Cook on medium for ten minutes, then test if it is your desired consistency
