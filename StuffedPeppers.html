    Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F and boil a large pot of water
  2. Prepare the peppers
    • Wash the peppers
    • Cut off the top
    • Hollow out the peppers
  3. Blanch the peppers
  4. Cook the vegetables
  5. cook the meat
  6. Add the tomatoes, seasoning and cooked rice
  7. Stir the desired amount of cheese into vegetables and meat mixture
  8. Stuff the mixture in the peppers
